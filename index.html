3. Artwork Consent & Non-Training Boundary

All character portraits and master illustrations in this archive are human-created artworks, not AI-generated images. Artists remain anonymous in this general disclosure; dedicated artist credit links alongside Master Character Artwork inside individual character drawers are planned for a later update.

Explicit Artist Permission: Character artwork across the roster (Kai'nek, Carnak, Carpo, Brutus, Abrax, Ph'ya, Eidos, Rhidos, and Rathen) has been explicitly allowed by the respective artists for display in this app.
Beta Pending Confirmation (Regal & Zahra): Only the artworks currently used for Regal and Zahra remain pending artist confirmation in this beta release. Should either artist decline or request changes or removal, those assets will be updated or removed promptly.
Zero Model Training / Improvement: The images provided to work with this application are not used to explicitly learn from, train, or improve any AI model—which CarpoPyronite regards as the most morally unacceptable misuse of artists' work. They exist strictly as static visual display assets within this project.
4. Discussion: Vibe-Coded Apps & AI Controversy

"Vibe-coded" and AI-assisted projects rightly draw skepticism across creative and technical communities. Common criticisms point to low-effort "AI slop" replacing real craft, obscured authorship claiming unverified credit, and—most seriously—generative models absorbing artists' work without consent.

This project takes a deliberate stance against those harms by separating creative authorship from engineering scaffolding. The characters, visual design direction, Canva architecture, worldbuilding rules, and lived testimonies did not come out of a prompt box—they belong to CarpoPyronite and consenting participants. Gemini 3.8 Flash (via Antigravity) and partial assistance from Grok 4.7 served purely to turn that human material into an interactive web interface across more than a hundred iterative back-and-forth passes.

Rather than concealing AI tooling or hand-waving asset provenance, this page documents the exact tools, transaction volume, engineering hours, consent boundaries, and pending beta permissions openly so visitors can engage with the project on honest terms.
